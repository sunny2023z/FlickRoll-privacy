FlickRoll (刷相册) does not collect, upload, or share any of your data. Your photos and your analysis results stay on your own device.

What data we handle

Photos: The app reads photos from your iPhone photo library in order to group them on your device by capture time, location, and visual similarity, so that you can go through them one by one and confirm what to keep or clean up.

Your choice records: The photo IDs you mark as "keep" or "to delete", along with the progress of your most recent organizing session, are stored on your device so that the app can resume where you left off.

How data is handled

All processing happens on your device. Specifically:

  • Photo grouping and image analysis run on your device; the photos themselves are never uploaded to any server.
  • Analysis results (the groups and similarity values) likewise exist only on your device.
  • The app has no account system; you do not need to register, sign in, or provide an email address or phone number.
  • The app contains no network request code and does not send any data off your device.
  • The app does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting services.

Deleting photos

The app moves selected photos to the system's "Recently Deleted" only after you confirm each one inside the app. The deletion flow has two confirmations:

  • A confirmation dialog inside the app;
  • The deletion authorization prompt shown by iOS.

Once photos are in the system's "Recently Deleted", they can be recovered within 30 days from the "Recently Deleted" album in the Photos app. The app never deletes silently and cannot bypass the system confirmation.

Basis: Apple's official support document support.apple.com/118558 (Recently Deleted keeps deleted items for 30 days).

That period is controlled by iOS and cannot be changed by the app.

Data storage and deletion

  • Your choice records are stored inside the app's sandbox on your device. Deleting the app removes them as well.
  • Photos are always managed by the iOS system photo library. The app holds no copies of your photos, and deleting the app does not affect your photos.

Children's privacy

The app is not designed for children under 13 and does not collect any personal information.

About permissions

The app requests the "Photos" permission (read and write). It is used to read the photos in the time range you select and group them by event, and to move selected photos into "Recently Deleted" after you confirm.

The system authorization prompt currently reads as follows. The app ships in Chinese, so the prompt is displayed in Chinese:

读取你选定范围内的照片供你筛选。只在本机处理、不上传；删除后可在系统的「最近删除」里恢复。

Translation: "Used to read photos within the time range you select and group similar photos on your device (for example, organizing 20 burst shots into 3 groups), so that you can review them one by one and confirm what to keep or clean up. Only after you confirm a second time does the app move the selected photos to the system's 'Recently Deleted'. Your photos are never uploaded."

If you choose "Selected Photos" access, the app only processes content within the scope you authorize; the features remain available.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, we will update this page inside the app and revise the "Last updated" date shown at the top.

「刷相册」不会收集、上传或分享你的任何数据。你的照片和分析结果始终留在你自己的设备上。

我们处理什么数据

照片：应用会读取你 iPhone 相册中的照片，用于在本机按拍摄时间、地点和画面相似度进行分组，供你逐张确认保留或清理。

你的选择记录：你标记为「保留」或「待删除」的照片 ID，以及你最近一次整理的进度，会保存在本机，用于下次打开时恢复进度。

数据如何处理

全部处理都在你的设备上完成。具体而言：

  • 照片的分组与画面分析在本机进行，照片本身不会被上传到任何服务器。
  • 分析结果（分组、相似度）同样只存在于本机。
  • 应用没有账号系统，你不需要注册、登录，也不需要提供邮箱或手机号。
  • 应用不包含任何网络请求代码，不从本机向外部发送数据。
  • 应用不使用任何第三方分析、广告或崩溃上报服务。

照片的删除

只有你在应用内逐张确认后，应用才会把选中的照片移到系统「最近删除」。删除流程有两道确认：

  • 应用内的确认弹窗；
  • iOS 系统弹出的删除授权确认。

照片进入系统「最近删除」后，可在 30 天内从「照片」应用的「最近删除」相簿中恢复。应用不会执行静默删除，也无法绕过系统确认。

依据：Apple 官方支持文档 support.apple.com/118558（Recently Deleted 保留删除项目 30 天）。

该期限由 iOS 系统控制，应用无法更改。

数据的存储与删除

  • 你的选择记录保存在本机应用沙盒内。删除应用即可一并清除。
  • 照片始终由 iOS 系统相册管理。应用不持有照片副本，删除应用不会影响你的照片。

儿童隐私

应用不面向 13 岁以下儿童设计，也不收集任何个人信息。

权限说明

应用申请「照片」权限（读取与修改），用途是读取你选择的照片并按事件分组，以及在你确认后把选中照片移入「最近删除」。

系统的授权提示文案为：

读取你选定范围内的照片供你筛选。只在本机处理、不上传；删除后可在系统的「最近删除」里恢复。

若你选择「部分照片」授权，应用只会处理你授权范围内的内容，功能仍然可用。

政策变更

若本政策有实质性变更，我们会在应用内更新本页并修改顶部的「最后更新」日期。
