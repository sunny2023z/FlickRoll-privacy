Frequently asked questions

Where do deleted photos go?

They are moved to the iOS system's "Recently Deleted", not erased. You can bring them back within 30 days from Photos → Albums → Recently Deleted. The app never deletes anything silently: it asks you to confirm, and then iOS asks you one more time.

Does the app need an internet connection?

No. All image analysis runs on your iPhone. The app contains no network request code, so your photos are never uploaded anywhere.

Does it read my entire photo library?

It only processes photos inside the time range you pick, and only within the access you grant. If you choose "Selected Photos", the app still works and simply limits itself to what you authorised.

Why weren't some similar photos grouped together?

Grouping uses burst information and on-device visual similarity, together with capture time and location. Photos taken in different places or at clearly different times are deliberately kept apart, so unrelated shots are not lumped in.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone running iOS 17.0 or later.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no account system — no sign-up, no sign-in, no email address required.

常见问题

删掉的照片去哪了？

被移到 iOS 系统的「最近删除」，不是永久抹掉。30 天内都可以在「照片 → 相簿 → 最近删除」里找回来。App 不会静默删除：先由你确认，iOS 还会再问你一次。

需要联网才能用吗？

不需要。所有画面分析都在你的 iPhone 上完成，App 不包含任何网络请求代码，照片不会被上传到任何地方。

它会读取我全部照片吗？

只会处理你选定的时间段内的照片，而且只在你授权的范围内。即使你选择「部分照片」，功能依然可用，只是范围限于你授权的内容。

为什么有些相似的照片没被分到一组？

分组依据连拍信息与本机的画面相似度，并结合拍摄时间和地点。拍摄地点相差较远、或时间明显不同的照片会被有意分开，避免把不相关的照片混在一起。

支持哪些设备？

运行 iOS 17.0 或更高版本的 iPhone。

需要注册账号吗？

不需要。App 没有账号系统，不用注册、不用登录，也不需要填邮箱。
