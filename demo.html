FlickRoll — Review Demo

Screen recordings provided for App Review. No account or sign-in is needed to use the app.

「刷相册」审核演示录屏。App 无账号系统，无需注册或登录。

What the recordings show
  • Granting photo access — including the "selected photos" option
  • Choosing a time range, then reviewing photos in groups
  • Swipe gestures: sideways to browse, up = to delete, down = keep
  • Undo, and the two-step confirmation before anything is deleted
  • The completion screen and the results view at the end of a session
录屏内容
  • 照片授权（含「部分照片」选项）
  • 选择时间段，然后按分组逐组过
  • 手势：左右浏览、上滑待删除、下滑保留
  • 撤销，以及删除前的两道确认
  • 完成页与整理结果页

Everything in these recordings happens on-device. The app has no network request code, so photos never leave the iPhone.

录屏中的全部处理都在设备本机完成。App 不含任何网络请求代码，照片不会离开 iPhone。
