FlickRoll — Review Demo

Screen recording provided for App Review.

Download the video (MP4)
What the recording shows:
  • Granting photo access (including the "selected photos" option)
  • Choosing a time range, then reviewing photos in groups
  • Swipe gestures: up = to delete, down = keep, sideways = browse
  • The two-step confirmation before anything is deleted
  • The results screen at the end of a session

No account, no sign-in, and no network access are required to use the app. Everything in this recording happens on-device.
